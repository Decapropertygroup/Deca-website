The Brief

One spa,
two tenancies,
two levels

Aurea Day Spa was taking two separate tenancies at Harbour Town, one on the ground floor and one upstairs, and turning them into a single business.

That meant building out treatment rooms, a reception and a retail space from open shells, to a design that had to feel like one place across two floors. Everything had to satisfy the centre's certifier before the doors could open.

The Work

What we did

  • 117 m² of partition walls across both tenancies, forming the treatment rooms and corridors
  • 44 m² of acoustic batten wall, so one treatment room cannot be heard from the next
  • 117 m² of plaster hung ceiling, flush set, with access panels where centre management directed
  • Eight aluminium cavity slider door frames supplied and installed
  • Plastering, setting, sanding and painting throughout to the specified colours
  • Form 12 and a Form 43 waterproofing certificate issued, with Forms 15 and 16 from every trade

Acoustic separation is the detail that matters most in a day spa. A treatment room that leaks noise into the next one is a room the business cannot charge full rate for.

The Finished Spa

From open shell to open doors

Deca built the walls, ceilings and openings. The fit-out styling, joinery and signage are the client's own.

The Result

Two tenancies,
one business

Both levels were handed over finished and certified, ready for the client's fit-out and styling to go in on top.

  • Both tenancies delivered to the same standard and finish
  • Acoustic separation between every treatment room
  • Form 12 issued, then extended to cover the upstairs tenancy
  • Form 43 waterproofing certificate issued
