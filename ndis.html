NDIS Construction

Building accessible, compliant spaces for NDIS participants. Housing modifications, SDA new builds and purpose-built facilities designed around real independence goals.

Our Approach

Built around people

Accessibility by Design

We start with the participant's needs, not the building's constraints. Every project is designed around real people, their mobility, independence goals and daily lived experience.

Standards Compliance

Deep familiarity with NDIS Practice Standards, SDA design requirements and NCC accessibility provisions. We build to meet them correctly, first time.

Participant-Centred

We collaborate with participants, support coordinators and plan managers throughout the entire project, not just at the brief. Clear communication at every stage.

What We Build

Our capabilities

  • Specialist Disability Accommodation (SDA) new builds, conversions and robust housing
  • Accessible bathroom and wet area modifications, including roll-in showers, grab rails and adjustable fixtures
  • Ramp construction, widened doorways and threshold removal for wheelchair access
  • Ceiling track hoist and mechanical lift system installation
  • Kitchen modifications for wheelchair accessibility and independent living
  • Sensory-friendly environments and low-stimulation design spaces
  • Community facility and day program space construction
  • Assistive technology integration, including smart home and environmental controls
  • Full compliance documentation aligned to NDIS Capital Supports funding
  • Minor modifications through to full SDA registered new builds
NDIS Funding

Working with Capital Supports

Navigating NDIS Capital Supports for construction can be complex. Deca helps prepare quotes and documentation aligned to NDIS requirements, making the process clear for participants and families.

We work with registered NDIS providers, support coordinators, occupational therapists, and directly with participants.
