The Brief

Work at height,
a mall that never shuts

Chevron Renaissance is a retail, dining and residential precinct on Surfers Paradise Boulevard, built around a three storey atrium under shade sails.

The decorative mouldings on the atrium bands and columns had failed and had to come off, with the facade behind them made good. Every metre of it was above shop entries and dining areas, and the centre had to open on time every morning with no trace of the night's work.

Night shift at height, looking down to the mall floor.

The Work

What we did

  • Decorative mouldings removed from the atrium bands and columns, working nights from a boom lift and on rope access
  • Hebel panels installed where the substrate behind the mouldings needed rebuilding
  • Rendered and painted at height to match the existing facade
  • The centre's tower repaired and repainted on rope access
  • Stainless steel fixings installed at height for the centre's Christmas display
  • Roof cleaning and a staged night washdown of the mall facades
On Site

Stripped, rebuilt, finished

The Result

Open every
morning

The work ran in stages over months of night shifts, and the centre traded every day of it. The same team was fitting out tenancies on the ground floor at the same time.

  • Shop entries sheeted every night against dust and debris
  • The mall walked and checked each morning before trading
  • Boom lift and rope access, so no scaffold ever stood in the mall
  • Tower, facade and roof works delivered for centre management on one program
