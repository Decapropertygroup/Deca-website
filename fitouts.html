Commercial Fit-Outs

Shops, offices, spas and hospitality fit-outs. We price it, build it and get it certified so the tenant can open on the date the centre set.

How a fit-out runs with us

1. Price

Send us the drawings and the fit-out guide. You get one fixed price, itemised line by line, so you can see exactly what's in it.

Free, no site fee
2. Program

We work back from your opening date and the centre's rules: trading hours, hoardings, inductions, noisy-work windows.

Built to the trading date
3. Build

Walls, ceilings, partitions, joinery, floors and paint. Every trade on one program, under one supervisor, with one point of contact for you and the centre.

Our own crews
4. Certify

Forms 15 and 16 collected as each trade finishes, so the certifier can issue the Form 12 and you can open.

Paperwork done as we go
5. Hand over

A defects walk with you before handover, and we come back for anything that turns up after.

Warranty in writing

The kind of spaces we fit out

Shops

New tenancies and refits in shopping centres, worked to the centre's fit-out guide and the date in the lease.

Offices

Partitions, glazed suites, ceilings and finishes, including floors that stay occupied while we work after hours.

Spas, bars and cafes

Wet areas, acoustic walls between treatment rooms, and the finishes the designer specified, built to pass the certifier first time.
