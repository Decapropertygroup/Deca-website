Division 02

Remedial & Roofing

Existing buildings, at height, on live sites. Roof waterproofing, facade repaint and remedial works delivered around tenants and trading hours, with daily reporting so you always know where the job is up to.

What We Do

Remedial capability

01

Roof Waterproofing & Membranes

Liquid-applied membrane systems over metal and concrete roofs, including primer, detailing to penetrations and upstands, and UV stable topcoats. Full system warranty on completion.

02

Facade Wash & Repaint

High-pressure wash, preparation, repair and repaint of commercial facades. Rope access and elevated work platforms where scaffold is not practical, on live and tenanted buildings.

03

Concrete & Ceiling Remedial

Spalling repair, crack injection, sealant renewal and ceiling make-safe and reinstatement. Work staged around tenants and trading hours to keep the building open.

04

Access & Make-Safe

Engineered scaffold, edge protection and access solutions for work at height, including towers, parapets and plant decks, with the make-safe works that have to come first.

How We Work

Nothing found on site
gets fixed without
a price first

Remedial work is the trade where surprises live. You cannot see what is under a roof sheet or behind a facade until the work is opened up, so we handle it the same way every time.

  • One fixed written price before we start, from a free site inspection
  • Anything found once the work is opened up is priced in writing for approval first
  • A daily progress report with photos for every day our crews are on site
  • Deca technicians working under Deca supervision, start to finish
  • A completion report and system warranty documentation on handover
