Remedial & Roofing

Roofs, facades, concrete and ceilings on buildings people are still using. Most of it done on rope or from a lift, worked around the tenants, with photo reports while we're on site.

What we fix

Roofs

Liquid membranes and coatings over metal and concrete roofs: clean, prime, detail every penetration and upstand, then the top coats. Warranted when it's done.

Facades

Wash, prep, repair and repaint. On rope or from an EWP where scaffold doesn't make sense, so the footpath and the shops underneath stay open.

Concrete and ceilings

Spalling, cracks, failed sealant, sagging or cracked ceilings. Made safe first, then fixed properly, staged around trading hours.

Access and make-safe

Scaffold, edge protection and rope access anchors for towers, parapets and plant decks, and the make-safe work that has to happen before anything else.

Nothing gets fixed without a price first

You can't see what's under a roof sheet or behind a facade until it's opened up. So we deal with it the same way every time.

  • A free look at the site, then one fixed price in writing
  • Anything we find once it's opened up gets priced in writing, and we wait for your OK
  • Progress reports with photos while we're on site
  • Our own crews, under our own supervisor
  • A completion report and the warranty paperwork at handover
