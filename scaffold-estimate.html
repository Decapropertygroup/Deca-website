What this is and is not

This is an estimate, not a quotation. Where the dimensions above have been entered correctly, we aim to bring the fixed price in within 10% of this figure once you send us drawings and we have looked at the site. It is generated from what you typed, so it is only as good as those numbers.

It assumes clear and level ground, unobstructed access, standard modular scaffold and normal working hours. Ground conditions, obstructions, staged handovers, permits, night work, heritage restrictions and anything hung, cantilevered or loaded will move it. Industrial and refinery work is not priced here, so call us for that.

An estimate produced here is not an offer and is not capable of acceptance. A fixed price is given only in a written quotation.
