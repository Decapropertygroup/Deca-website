Project

Paparazzi, Chevron Renaissance

A new retail fit-out for fashion retailer Paparazzi in the Chevron Renaissance Shopping Centre, Surfers Paradise, delivered to a trading date set by centre management.

Commercial Fit-Out Surfers Paradise, QLD Retail Completed 2025
The Brief

A shop that had
to open on time

Centre management came to us with an approved design, an existing quote from another contractor, and a tenancy sitting empty. Every week it stayed empty was a week the centre and the tenant were both losing.

We priced it against the incumbent quote and won the job on price and programme. The owner, Nathan Arden, needed a finished store. The centre needed it handed over clean, certified, and without disruption to the tenancies either side of it.

The Work

What we did

  • Full tenancy fit-out delivered from the approved design, concept through to handover
  • Walls, curved bulkhead ceilings and shopfront works, set, sanded and painted throughout
  • Every trade coordinated under Deca, with one point of contact for the tenant and the centre
  • Work staged around neighbouring tenancies trading either side of us
  • Form 16 and Form 12 issued and handed to the certifier and centre management

The paperwork is the part that trips most fit-outs up. A store cannot open until the certifier is satisfied, so we treat the forms as part of the job rather than something chased afterwards.

The tenancy went from an approved drawing to a trading store, with the compliance documentation in the certifier's hands rather than on a list of things still to come.
